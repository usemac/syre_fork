## Key Decisions

- **PMdim/PMNc resize**: Added explicit resizing in `define_machine.m` because `syreDefaultMotor.mat` has `nlay=1` but `data0` iterates `1:geo.nlay`, causing index out-of-bounds crash in `PMdefinition_Seg`.
- **Barrier reduction**: Required 3 rounds of reduction (`ALPHApu` from [0.65,0.40] → [0.50,0.30]; `HCpu` from [0.50,0.35] → [0.35,0.25]) to fit barriers within the rotor.
- **Operating point**: Kept (x=0.60, b=0.52) throughout; PF remained 0.590.
- **Final geometry**: `dalpha=[16.5,10.5]` mech deg (27° total, 3° iron margin), `hc_pu=[0.27,0.44]`, `dx(2)=0.640`, `pontT=1.00 mm`.

## Known Issues

- **Shaft radius warning**: `Ar=34.75 mm > ArLim` appears for most (x,b) cells — cosmetic for this design, requires shaft reduction for small-radius designs.
- **hc_pu is NOT final**: `calcHcCheckGeoControlwDx` recomputes `hc` per-cell from available radial space. Final `hc_pu=[0.27,0.44]` differs from input `[0.35,0.25]`.
- **FEAfix not completed**: `cli_run_xb_feafix` was started but aborted (FEMM-heavy, ~30+ min).
